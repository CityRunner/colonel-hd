The first release of colonel-hd: The Colonel's Bequest from the start to
the ending, on a new SCI0 interpreter, in HD or the original look.

- The whole game plays as the original: a scripted playthrough gives the
  same kernel calls, screens, actors, parser results and music as ScummVM
  from the boot to the last screen.
- HD mode: the backgrounds are vector art at any whole-number scale (1 to
  16); sprites and text are the original pixels, enlarged. F12 switches to
  the original look.
- Music and sound on an emulated AdLib (Nuked OPL3) or Roland MT-32
  (mt32emu, with your own ROMs).
- The game's own dialogs save and restore (`saves/colonel-hd-001.sav` and
  on).
- One data folder for the configuration, the game, the ROMs, the saves and
  the cache: the unpacked archive itself, or `--data <folder>`, or your
  user folder (see README.md, "Where the program keeps its files").
- `colonel-hd.ini` is changed only where you changed something, and keeps
  your comments.

Since the release candidate 0.9: the project is renamed colonel-hd (old
`sierra-hd.ini` and saves are not read; rename them by hand), the data
folder, the ini updated in place, `--log` for more messages, fixes for
malformed script and song resources found by fuzzing, and a reproducible
build.

Binaries for Linux (glibc 2.38 or newer) and Windows, x64 and ARM64.
Bring your own copy of the game: copy its files into `game/` of the
unpacked archive. See README.md in the archive.
